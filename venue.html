Venue & Local Information

The SECURE-AI4H workshop will be hosted in Singapore, a global hub for research, innovation, and healthcare. Explore travel details, accommodation options, and everything you need to plan your visit.

Visa Information

Many participants will travel internationally to attend the workshop in Singapore and may need to obtain a travel visa. We strongly recommend that attendees review the relevant requirements and begin the visa application process early.

Visa requirements depend on your country of citizenship and current residence. Please consult the official guidelines from the Singapore authorities and your local consulate to determine whether a visa is required and which documents are needed.

Participants who require an invitation letter for visa purposes should refer to the Registration page for details on how to request this document after completing their registration.

For the most accurate and up-to-date information, please refer to the official immigration resources for Singapore and the AAAI conference website.

Hotel Information

AAAI has reserved a block of rooms at several hotels in Singapore at reduced conference rates. Space is limited, and we strongly encourage participants to make their bookings well in advance.

Cutoff Dates

Reservation cutoff dates vary by hotel. Discounted conference rates and room availability cannot be guaranteed after the stated cutoff dates.

Cancellation Policies

Cancellation policies differ by property. Please review the terms carefully at the time of booking to avoid unexpected fees.
