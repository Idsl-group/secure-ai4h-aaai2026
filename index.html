About the Workshop

The SECURE-AI4H workshop brings together researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and health to explore methods that are safe, ethical, certified, uncertainty-aware, robust, and explainable.

Why this workshop?

As AI systems become increasingly integrated into healthcare workflows, we must ensure that they behave reliably under uncertainty, respect ethical constraints, and are interpretable to clinicians, patients, and regulators.

Who should attend?

We invite researchers, clinicians, engineers, policymakers, and industry partners interested in deploying trustworthy AI tools across the health spectrum, from early-stage research to clinical translation.

Workshop Highlights

Trustworthy AI for Health

Methods for uncertainty quantification, robustness, interpretability, and safety in clinical AI systems.

Human–AI Collaboration

Designing interactive AI tools that support clinicians in decision-making while respecting clinical workflows and constraints.

Evaluation & Certification

Frameworks for evaluating, certifying, and monitoring AI systems in real-world healthcare environments.
